# Source policy and checkpoint boundary

Verified snapshot date: 10 September 2026.

## Authoritative sources

1. [ELTE IK BSc Thesis consultations - deadlines 2024](https://www.inf.elte.hu/dstore/document/227123/BSc%20Thesis%20consultations%20-%20deadlines%202024%20.pdf) defines checkpoint 5 and states that it is outside Thesis Consultation course fulfillment but necessary for upload and the final examination.
2. [Assessment Criteria for BSc Theses at ELTE IK](https://www.inf.elte.hu/dstore/document/227115/thesis_assessment.docx) defines the seven later assessment areas and zero-area rule. Use it to prevent an unassessable final package, not to calculate points.
3. [Gregory Morse's academic homepage](https://grm.web.elte.hu/) under **Supervision -> BSc Thesis Workflow Guidance** supplies final structure, page minima, bibliography, complex-feature, exam-topic, repository, testing, AI, Turnitin, package, and demo requirements.
4. [Gregory Morse's updated Bachelor Thesis Review](https://grm.web.elte.hu/supervision/thesis_review_point_by_point_updated.docx) gives the supervisor's point-by-point final review calibration. Do not fill or score it in this checkpoint audit.

Use no other source to create ELTE or supervisor process requirements. External technical sources may verify student claims or citations.

## Precedence and freshness

- Current binding ELTE rule precedes supervisor convention.
- The official checkpoint description governs the stage boundary.
- Specific final-submission guidance precedes general advice.
- An explicit, evidenced supervisor-approved exception applies only to that student.
- If a live source materially changed, use the current applicable text and disclose the change.

## Official schedule

The first date is for a January final examination; the second is for a June final examination.

| Checkpoint | January exam | June exam |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 Topic registration and work plan | 15 May | 15 October |
| 2 Requirements planning | 1 July | 1 December |
| 3 Architecture | 10 September | 10 February |
| 4 Implementation at 60-70% | 1 October | 1 March |
| 5 Completed thesis | 10 November | 10 April |

Ask which exam session and year apply. The official document recommends a mock defense one to two weeks before the final examination.

For students uploading by 1 December / 1 May, the official document gives 15 December / 15 May as the Neptun consultation-grade entry deadline. Otherwise the ordinary practical-grade deadline is 7 January / 7 June. A later exceptional completion requires vice-dean permission for documented illness or extraordinary problems.

## Assessment boundary

Return completed-thesis checkpoint sufficiency and separate timing only. Do not calculate the 30-point score, assign a grade, or issue the supervisor's formal support/do-not-support declaration. The assessment form says that a zero in any one of seven areas makes the overall score zero; therefore every area must at least be genuinely assessable for a Sufficient checkpoint result.

## Submission routes from the 2026 supervisor snapshot

- Normal route: follow the current Neptun submission instructions and package rules.
- Deferred route for a student not submitting in Neptun that semester: the snapshot describes a three-week extension and a ZIP to the supervisor before the execution demo containing the thesis PDF, complete source, and signed AI declaration. Verify that this option still applies before relying on it.

Do not assume a student wants to defer or avoid absolutorium. Record the intended route.

## AI and Turnitin

Current supervisor guidance requires a signed AI-use declaration as a separate file in the submission ZIP, accurate listing of every tool and its purpose/method/location, and point-of-use documentation such as thesis footnotes and code comments. False or contradictory declarations can block evaluation. Audit consistency; do not infer AI use from style or use AI detectors.

The supervisor snapshot allows one student pre-check upload to Turnitin and warns that repeated uploads may delay reports. Treat a similarity percentage as a review signal, never as proof of plagiarism or AI use.

## Safe verification

Prefer a live student-run demo or safe local/test configuration. Never require production credentials or execute actions that mutate remote services, incur charges, expose private data, or damage a system. If safe verification is unavailable, mark the execution gate Unverified.
