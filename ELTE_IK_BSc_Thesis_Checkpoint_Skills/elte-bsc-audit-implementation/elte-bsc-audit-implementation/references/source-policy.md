# Source policy and checkpoint boundary

Verified snapshot date: 10 September 2026.

## Authoritative sources

1. [ELTE IK BSc Thesis consultations - deadlines 2024](https://www.inf.elte.hu/dstore/document/227123/BSc%20Thesis%20consultations%20-%20deadlines%202024%20.pdf) requires presentation of an implementation at least 60-70 percent ready and discussion of final-exam topics.
2. [Assessment Criteria for BSc Theses at ELTE IK](https://www.inf.elte.hu/dstore/document/227115/thesis_assessment.docx) calibrates implementation, testing, and program-execution evidence. Do not assign its points here.
3. [Gregory Morse's academic homepage](https://grm.web.elte.hu/) under **Supervision -> BSc Thesis Workflow Guidance** supplies repository, debugging, documentation, testing, reproducibility, AI, and exam-topic expectations.

Use no other source to create ELTE or supervisor process requirements. External technical sources may verify student claims.

## Precedence and freshness

- Current binding ELTE rule precedes supervisor convention.
- Official checkpoint description precedes final-thesis criteria.
- Specific stage guidance precedes general advice.
- An explicit, evidenced supervisor-approved scope change or exception applies only to that student.
- If a live source materially changed, use the current applicable text and disclose the change.

## Official schedule and course boundary

The first date is for a January final examination; the second is for a June final examination.

| Checkpoint | January exam | June exam |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 Topic registration and work plan | 15 May | 15 October |
| 2 Requirements planning | 1 July | 1 December |
| 3 Architecture | 10 September | 10 February |
| 4 Implementation at 60-70% | 1 October | 1 March |
| 5 Completed thesis | 10 November | 10 April |

Checkpoint 4 is the last content milestone used for entering the Thesis Consultation grade in Neptun. The official course result also depends on timing and process. Return checkpoint sufficiency and deadline status, not Excellent/Satisfactory/Fail, a 0-30 thesis score, or a predicted grade.

For students uploading the thesis by 1 December / 1 May, the official document gives 15 December / 15 May as the Neptun grade-entry deadline. Otherwise the ordinary practical-grade deadline is 7 January / 7 June. A later exceptional completion requires the vice-dean's permission for documented illness or extraordinary problems; do not infer that exception.

## Safe verification

Prefer a live student-run demonstration, recorded demonstration, or safe local test environment. Never require production credentials. Do not execute commands that can mutate remote services, incur charges, expose private data, or damage a system. If safe execution is unavailable, mark execution-dependent gates Unverified rather than trusting screenshots or prose.

## AI and integrity

Current supervisor guidance requires point-of-use documentation, including inline comments for substantial AI-generated code included with minimal modification, and an accurate final declaration. At checkpoint 4, check that a usable record is being maintained. Do not infer AI use from style or use AI detectors.

## Documentation timing

The supervisor's final target is at least ten pages for the Implementation chapter and ten pages for Testing. At the 60-70 percent checkpoint, require substantive current documentation and a credible completion path, but do not invent a proportional page minimum. Report a final-page-count risk as non-blocking unless the current documentation is too absent to evidence implementation decisions.
