# Source policy and checkpoint boundary

Verified snapshot date: 10 September 2026.

## Authoritative sources

1. [ELTE IK BSc Thesis consultations - deadlines 2024](https://www.inf.elte.hu/dstore/document/227123/BSc%20Thesis%20consultations%20-%20deadlines%202024%20.pdf) defines the five consultation milestones and paired January/June final-exam deadlines.
2. [Assessment Criteria for BSc Theses at ELTE IK](https://www.inf.elte.hu/dstore/document/227115/thesis_assessment.docx) supplies later-thesis calibration, especially the solution-plan criteria. Do not score against it at checkpoint 2.
3. [Gregory Morse's academic homepage](https://grm.web.elte.hu/) under **Supervision -> BSc Thesis Workflow Guidance** supplies supervisor-specific planning requirements.

Use no other source to create ELTE or supervisor process requirements. External technical sources may be consulted only to verify student claims or citations.

## Precedence and freshness

- Current binding ELTE rule precedes supervisor convention.
- Official checkpoint description precedes later final-thesis criteria.
- Dedicated stage rule precedes general website advice.
- An explicit, evidenced supervisor-approved exception applies to that student only.
- If a live source materially changed, use its current applicable text and disclose the change.
- Do not generalize a semester-specific exception. The 2026 snapshot mentioned a December 14 grace date for one planning cycle; otherwise use 1 July / 1 December.

## Official schedule

The first date is for a January final examination; the second is for a June final examination.

| Checkpoint | January exam | June exam |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 Topic registration and work plan | 15 May | 15 October |
| 2 Requirements planning | 1 July | 1 December |
| 3 Architecture | 10 September | 10 February |
| 4 Implementation at 60-70% | 1 October | 1 March |
| 5 Completed thesis | 10 November | 10 April |

Ask which exam session and year apply. Do not choose a date from the current date alone.

## Assessment boundary

Return checkpoint content sufficiency and separate deadline status. Do not issue the official consultation grade, a 0-30 thesis score, a predicted grade, or a defense-support decision. The official three-point consultation grade also considers milestone timing. Checkpoint 4 is its last content milestone; checkpoint 5 is outside course fulfillment.

## Planning versus final design

The official checkpoint requires the software requirements, especially functional requirements, using detailed use cases or user stories. Gregory Morse additionally requires a detailed specification that already records core design decisions and can be reused in the final Design chapter. Do not fail checkpoint 2 merely because the full architecture, mandatory class diagram, ten design pages, or final code does not yet exist; those belong to later checkpoints.

## AI and integrity

Audit disclosed AI use for accurate attribution, point-of-use documentation, and project-specific technical grounding when applicable. Do not infer AI use from writing style, use an AI detector, or equate a similarity percentage with misconduct.
