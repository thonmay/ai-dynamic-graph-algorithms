# Source policy and checkpoint boundary

Verified snapshot date: 10 September 2026.

## Authoritative sources

1. [ELTE IK BSc Thesis consultations - deadlines 2024](https://www.inf.elte.hu/dstore/document/227123/BSc%20Thesis%20consultations%20-%20deadlines%202024%20.pdf) defines the five consultation milestones and paired January/June final-exam deadlines.
2. [Assessment Criteria for BSc Theses at ELTE IK](https://www.inf.elte.hu/dstore/document/227115/thesis_assessment.docx) defines seven later 30-point assessment areas. Use it only for future-risk calibration in checkpoint 1.
3. [Gregory Morse's academic homepage](https://grm.web.elte.hu/) under **Supervision -> BSc Thesis Workflow Guidance** supplies the supervisor-specific requirements.
4. [BSc Thesis Topic Proposal Prompt](https://grm.web.elte.hu/supervision/BScThesisTopicProposalPrompt.md) supplies the dedicated checkpoint-1 proposal format.

Use no other source to create ELTE or supervisor process requirements. External technical sources may be consulted only to verify a student's technical claims or citations.

## Precedence and freshness

- Apply a current binding ELTE rule before a supervisor convention.
- Apply the official checkpoint description before later general thesis criteria.
- Apply a dedicated stage rule before general website advice.
- Apply an explicit, evidenced supervisor-approved exception to that student only.
- If a live source has materially changed since the snapshot, use the current applicable text and disclose the change.
- Do not generalize a semester-specific exception. The 2026 snapshot mentioned a December 14 grace date for one planning cycle; the official checkpoint-2 deadline remains 1 July / 1 December unless that extension is confirmed for the audited cohort.

## Official schedule

The first date is for a January final examination; the second is for a June final examination.

| Checkpoint | January exam | June exam |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 Topic registration and work plan | 15 May | 15 October |
| 2 Requirements planning | 1 July | 1 December |
| 3 Architecture | 10 September | 10 February |
| 4 Implementation at 60-70% | 1 October | 1 March |
| 5 Completed thesis | 10 November | 10 April |

Ask which exam session and year apply. Do not choose a date from the current date alone.

## Assessment boundary

Return only checkpoint content sufficiency and a separate deadline status. Do not issue:

- the official Thesis Consultation course result or grade;
- a 0-30 thesis score;
- a predicted final grade;
- a formal support/do-not-support defense decision.

The official consultation course uses Excellent/Satisfactory/Fail and includes process/timeliness. Checkpoint 4 is the last milestone used for entering that grade. Checkpoint 5 is outside fulfillment of the consultation course, though necessary for thesis upload and the final examination.

## AI and integrity

The supervisor's 2026 guidance permits AI use subject to current faculty policy, requires accurate disclosure and point-of-use documentation, and places responsibility on the student. At checkpoint 1, audit any disclosed AI assistance for transparency and technical specificity, but do not demand the final signed declaration early unless current policy does. Never infer AI use from prose style or use an AI detector.
