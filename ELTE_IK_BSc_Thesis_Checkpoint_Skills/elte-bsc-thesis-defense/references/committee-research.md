# Committee research and thesis-specific questions

Use when the defense's assigned committee is available, or to explain exactly what tailoring remains unavailable.

## Establish the actual panel

Start with a supplied current assignment email/Neptun notice containing names and roles. Do not ask for the student's password or access another student's private notice. An institutionally issued assignment for this student's sitting is more specific than an older public pool. Record date, programme/language, specialization and sitting if known.

Cross-check https://www.inf.elte.hu/zarovizsga-bizottsagok and the appropriate supervisor guide:

- BSc: https://grm.web.elte.hu/supervision/BScExamCommittee.md
- MSc: https://grm.web.elte.hu/supervision/MScExamCommittee.md

Read dated addenda/corrections before relying on old sections. A public committee pool does not prove attendance at this student's defense. Never import a previous student's panel or infer a member from an unnamed "external" slot. Do not transfer a Hungarian programme's panel to an English sitting without evidence. If the public page and assignment differ, explain which applies and ask only if the assignment itself is ambiguous.

## Reuse or build the audit

Reuse a supplied audit after checking identity, date, coverage and cited evidence. Update consequential gaps rather than repeating sound research. When absent, build a focused committee audit yourself using the supervisor's guide as a starting index and official institutional profiles, teaching/syllabus pages and relevant primary publications as evidence.

For each assigned member record:

| Field | What to retain |
| --- | --- |
| Identity and role | Name variants, department/institution, supplied or official institutional email/profile link if useful for disambiguation; assignment source |
| Verified expertise | Relevant stated research/teaching, with direct source and access date |
| Thesis connection | Specific contribution, method, architecture, proof or evaluation detail this expertise could illuminate |
| Practice angle | A reasoned question or explanation to rehearse; explicitly an inference |
| Limits | Stale/missing profiles, homonyms, uncertain authorship/role, unavailable sources |

Match names using affiliation and linked author identifiers before attributing publications. A coauthor listing alone is weak evidence of a person's specific contribution; teaching adjacent courses is not proof of a future exam question. Do not manufacture member email addresses or require them if identity is already clear.

Do not present anonymous ratings, personality labels, star rankings or historical predicted probabilities as facts about the examiner or the upcoming defense. If an old guide includes them, prefer its substantiated research/teaching information and dated corrections. Keep the preparation respectful and grounded in subject matter.

If a member cannot be identified confidently, mark that member unverified and provide role/subject-based practice without speculative biography. If browsing fails, use a supplied dated audit provisionally and disclose that verification limit. Lack of a committee audit is work for the assistant, not a reason to ask the student to write one.

## Tailor while preserving the academic argument

Adapt explanations, diagram clarity, backup slides and question emphasis to demonstrated interests that actually overlap the thesis. Preserve all central contributions and limitations. Do not alter evidence, overfill slides with unrelated research, flatter examiners or hide weaknesses.

The brief can prioritize likely useful topics without assigning numeric question probabilities. In defense-questions.md distinguish:

- **Reviewer question:** quoted or faithfully paraphrased from supplied review, with source.
- **Core thesis practice:** warranted by the work regardless of panel.
- **Committee-linked practice inference:** motivated by verified expertise; never labelled an actual past or certain future question.

For each question provide practical answer checkpoints and an evidence anchor. Where no answer is supported, say what is unknown and what a defensible response can acknowledge. Do not invent past-question provenance. Give follow-ups that test explanation, alternatives, failure boundaries and evidence strength rather than memorization.

BSc emphasis: contribution ownership, architecture, data/algorithm choices, acceptance/testing, reliability and trade-offs. MSc emphasis: research framing, related work, originality, assumptions, methodology, comparison validity, uncertainty, reproducibility and significance. Include relevant registered-subject connections without turning the pack into the separate professional exam.
