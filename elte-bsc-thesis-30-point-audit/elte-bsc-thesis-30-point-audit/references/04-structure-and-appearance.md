# Structure, language, and appearance

Use the editable source for substance and the submitted PDF for authoritative final appearance. Cross-check them before scoring.

## C2.1 Compulsory parts

Verify the thesis includes the approved registration/proposal form, user documentation, developer documentation, bibliography, and table of contents. Under the supervisor's recommended structure, also inventory cover data, introduction, conclusion, lists of figures/tables where applicable, and appendices. Optional parts such as abstract or acknowledgements cannot be demanded as official scored requirements unless a current rule makes them mandatory.

The AI declaration is currently required as a separate package file. Treat it as an eligibility gate, not as automatically satisfying or failing C2.1.

## C2.2 Logical structure and consistency

Check:

- hierarchy, section purpose, and coherent progression;
- consistent terminology, project scope, feature names, actors, interfaces, data model, and conclusions;
- transitions and cross-references among proposal, user docs, design, realization, testing, and conclusion;
- separation of user and developer concerns;
- whether design states intended structure while implementation records realized decisions without redundant copying;
- whether significant deviation from the supervisor's recommended two-part structure was approved and remains coherent.

Supervisor targets checked 2026-09-10: introduction 3-5 pages, user documentation at least 20 pages, design/implementation/testing at least 10 pages each, conclusion 2-4 pages, typical strong total 60-70 pages and 70-80 potentially supporting very high quality. Enforce the stated minima as supervisor requirements, but do not award structure based on length alone.

## C2.3 Editing and final appearance

### Mechanical pass

Inspect PDF metadata, page count/sizes/orientation, embedded fonts, text extraction, bounding boxes, links, bookmarks, and image resolution where possible. Measure content bounds by page and flag margins inconsistent with the current required template, clipping, content outside the media/crop box, unusual page-size changes, and outlier headers/footers. If no authoritative numeric margin exists, report measured margins and consistency; do not invent one.

### Visual pass

Render every page. Inspect all pages in manageable batches or contact sheets, then inspect every uncertain/flagged page at higher resolution. Check:

- title/front matter and proposal page fidelity;
- margins, page breaks, blank pages, widows/orphans, clipping, overlaps, glyphs, and hyphenation;
- heading hierarchy, numbering, TOC page references, running heads, page numbers, captions, and cross-references;
- table/figure/code/equation legibility, scale, balance, source attribution, and placement;
- bibliography layout and appendix transitions;
- placeholders, broken references, unresolved change markup, comments, or editor artifacts.

For text-heavy diagrams, check vector quality or equivalent zoom legibility. A visually attractive PDF with broken numbering or unreadable evidence does not earn the point.

## C2.4 Academic language and sources

Perform an exhaustive reference-integrity pass:

1. inventory in-text citations and bibliography entries;
2. confirm every citation resolves and every bibliography item is used or justified;
3. detect missing fields, duplicates, inconsistent styles, broken URLs/DOIs, and uncited copied figures/tables/code;
4. verify bibliographic metadata and current technical sources against primary/official sources when internet access permits;
5. inspect high-impact factual, novelty, quantitative, legal/security, and methodology claims for adequate support;
6. distinguish quotation, paraphrase, common knowledge, student result, and unsupported claim.

Current supervisor guidance requires at least 30 bibliography items and strongly recommends 50; sources may include papers, books, standards, RFCs, libraries, tools, platforms, and documentation actually used. Fewer than 30 is a supervisor-requirement failure relevant to C2.4. Quantity does not compensate for irrelevant, low-quality, or unused entries.

Check grammar and academic register across the full thesis, including captions, tables, UI text quoted in screenshots, and code-listing explanations. Identify patterns with representative precise examples rather than flooding the report with every typo; provide a separate exhaustive issue list when useful.

Do not infer plagiarism or AI authorship from prose style. Use Turnitin only through the official human workflow and record the supplied report status without pretending to reproduce it.
