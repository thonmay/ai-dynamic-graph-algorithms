## REPAIR_HINTS

In `_is_valid_lexbfs`, the line:

```python
U &= ~adj_vb
```

should be:

```python
U &= adj_vb
```

This is the single-line fix. The logic: U should contain a's that have NOT yet been "unwitnessed" (i.e., for all b' in (a, current_b), a-b' in E). When processing b, the a's in U that are NOT adjacent to b (T = U & ~adj_vb) become unwitnessed at b — we check the 4-point property for them. After checking, they should be removed from U (they're handled). The a's in U that ARE adjacent to b should stay in U (b didn't create a violation). So U &= adj_vb (keep only those adjacent to b).

Additionally, the "check only the minimum a in T" optimization may also be unsound (the monotonicity argument in the docstring is questionable), but the primary bug is the inverted U update. After fixing U &= adj_vb, the verifier should correctly detect the violation at (a=0, b=3, c=1) and trigger a recompute.
