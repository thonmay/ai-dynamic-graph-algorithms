\chapter{Submission and Disclosure Checklist}
\label{app:declarations}

\TemplateGuidance{This checklist is authoring support, not a substitute for the
official current ELTE IK instructions. Delete this appendix if your supervisor wants
the checklist kept outside the thesis.}

\begin{itemize}
  \item The cover metadata exactly matches the approved topic registration.
  \item The approved topic form is included where required.
  \item The PDF and editable source were generated from the same final revision.
  \item The complete source code and reproducible manifests are enclosed.
  \item All three contributions and all approved commitments are traceable to evidence.
  \item Exactly three approved exam topics are integrated naturally in the thesis.
  \item Every borrowed idea, quotation, figure, dataset, tool, library, and platform is
        cited or attributed as appropriate.
  \item AI assistance is disclosed at the point of use where required, and the signed
        ELTE IK AI declaration is supplied as a separate submission file.
  \item No credentials, personal data, private keys, or prohibited confidential material
        appear in the thesis or source package.
  \item \texttt{\string\showtemplateguidancefalse} is set in \texttt{config.tex}; no
        placeholders, TODOs, broken links, warnings, or unreadable figures remain.
\end{itemize}
